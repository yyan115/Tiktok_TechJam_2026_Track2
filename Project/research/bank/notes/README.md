# Note format

Keep each authoritative note as one flat, lowercase-named `.md` file in this
directory, UTF-8 with LF newlines and no more than 128 KiB. A useful note says:

- what primary source was inspected and where;
- the exact bounded claim the source supports (or contradicts);
- how that claim relates to KuaiRand-Pure and the official within-user metric;
- important limitations or alternative explanations; and
- what experiment could falsify the proposed use of the claim.

The catalog may cite a nonblank range of at most 12 lines and 4,000 UTF-8
bytes. The controller—not the attempt card—extracts and hashes that range.
